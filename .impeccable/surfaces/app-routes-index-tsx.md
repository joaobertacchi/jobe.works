# Home — jobe.works

## Scope and visitor mode

- Surface: the localized homepage route (`app/routes/$locale._index.tsx`), the entry surface of the JOBE marketing site. Persuade mode: the visitor decides whether to explore services and book a Product Readiness Call.

## Audience, job, action/task

- Founders and technical leaders of startups/SMBs with an AI product that gained traction but is not production-safe, or teams needing senior engineering direction.
- Job on this surface: get to know the company and the services; the primary action is exploring services and the StockCast case, with booking a Product Readiness Call as the conversion point.
- Proof/content that earns belief: the StockCast case study; the diagnostic-first method (call, diagnosis, directed offer).

## Constraints

- Canon visual world (owner-chosen 2026-08-15): category-standard engineering-consultancy arrangement at Linear/Stripe craft level; restrained, white space, hairline rules, neo-grotesque type, deep engineering-blue accent (#1d3f8f).
- Static site, en + pt-BR, ADR-governed; no invented prices, testimonials, benchmarks, or customers.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

- Approved composition: comp-a (Structured Standard), approved 2026-08-15 via the comp decision page; comp file `.impeccable/mocks/comp-a.webp`.
- Memorable moment: the hero's technical isometric motif beside the left-aligned 'Engineering that Works' headline — must read as a signature, not decoration.

## Unresolved decisions

- Real case-study material for StockCast (charts, metrics) is not in the repository; may be authored synthetically and labeled, or placed as clearly marked placeholders.
- Contact-form integration provider and analytics provider.
- Final page set beyond home/services/case/contact and its navigation order.
